FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before you start recording.

Is FitUp free to use?
Yes. The core features — route tracking, social posts, follows, GPX import and export — are free.
What activities can I track?
FitUp is built for outdoor activities like running, cycling, walking, and hiking — anything where a GPS route tells the story.
Will it keep recording when my screen is off?
Yes. As long as a recording session is active, FitUp keeps logging in the background. A visible notification stays on your device so you always know it's running.
Can I import or export my routes?
Absolutely. FitUp supports GPX import and export, so you can bring routes in from other apps or take your data with you.
How do I sign in?
You can register with an email and password, use a one-time code sent to your inbox, or sign in with Google. Authentication tokens are stored in your device's secure storage.
How do I delete my account?
You can delete your account from the app's settings at any time. Your personal profile data is removed within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.
